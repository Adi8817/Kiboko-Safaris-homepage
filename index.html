Here are just a few of the amazing animals you can see on an African Wildlife safari

  • Lions: These majestic predators are the kings of the savanna. They can be seen hunting in prides, or relaxing in the shade of a tree.
  • Elephants: These gentle giants are the largest land animals on Earth. They can be seen grazing in herds, or bathing in the waterhole.
  • Giraffes: These long-necked animals are the tallest land mammals on Earth. They can be seen browsing for leaves in the trees, or drinking from the watering hole.
  • Rhinos: These armored animals are the second-largest land mammals on Earth. They can be seen grazing in the grasslands, or bathing in the mud.
  • Leopards: These stealthy predators are often seen perched in trees, waiting for their prey to come within reach.
  • Cheetahs: These fastest land animals on Earth can reach speeds of up to 70 miles per hour. They can be seen hunting gazelles and other small mammals.
  • Hippopotamuses: These aggressive animals are the third-largest land mammals on Earth. They can be seen grazing in the water, or sunbathing on the banks of the river.
  • Monkeys: These playful animals can be seen swinging through the trees, or foraging for food on the ground.
  • Birds: Africa is home to an incredible variety of birds, from the colorful flamingos of the Serengeti to the majestic eagles of the mountains.
